COLUMBUS, OH – May 21, 2026 – Lower , a modern homeownership platform built to help everyone achieve financial freedom, today announced Paul Zinn as EVP of Retail Lending and Divisional Manager. Zinn joins Lower at an important stage in the company’s retail expansion as it continues investing in its platform, leadership, and national growth strategy. He will report directly to Craig Montgomery, Lower’s Chief Strategy Officer and Head of Retail Lending.
