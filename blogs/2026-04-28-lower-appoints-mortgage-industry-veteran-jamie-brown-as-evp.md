COLUMBUS, OH -- April 28, 2026 -- Lower , a modern homeownership platform built to help everyone achieve financial freedom, today announced the appointment of Jamie Brown as EVP, Divisional President. In this role, Brown will focus on building and scaling a high-performing, Southeast-focused division with a national reach, as the company continues to expand its presence and invest in top-tier talent.
